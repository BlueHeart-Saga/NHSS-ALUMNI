# Non-Technical School Administrator User Manual

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the **ABC School Alumni Management Portal**. This user manual explains how school administrators and batch coordinators can manage alumni applications, organize reunions, scan tickets, and broadcast announcements without needing technical expertise.

---

## 2. Core Administrative Workflows

### 2.1 Verifying Pending Alumni Registrations
1. Log in to the Admin Portal (`http://admin.abcschool.edu`).
2. Click **Verification Queue** in the left sidebar.
3. Review applicant details (Name, Photo, Batch, Admission Number, Mobile, Email).
4. Click **Approve** to grant full alumni access, or **Reject** if records do not match.

### 2.2 Importing School Roster via CSV
1. Navigate to **Alumni Directory** -> Click **Import CSV Roster**.
2. Upload your school excel/CSV roster containing columns: `Name, Batch, Admission Number, Mobile, Email`.
3. View the summary box showing Total Rows, Auto-Approved, Duplicates Flagged, and Errors.
4. If errors exist, click **Download Error CSV** to inspect invalid rows.

### 2.3 Creating & Publishing a Get-Together Event
1. Click **Events & Get-Togethers** -> Click **Create Get-Together**.
2. Fill in Event Name, Target Batch (or School-wide), Date, Start/End Time, Venue Name, Address, and Maximum Capacity.
3. Select whether alumni can bring adult/child guests.
4. Click **Publish Event Immediately** to broadcast to alumni mobile apps.

### 2.4 Venue QR Ticket Check-in Procedure
1. On the event day, open **QR Check-in Terminal**.
2. Select the active venue event from the dropdown.
3. Point your tablet/mobile camera or optical scanner at the alumnus's mobile screen ticket.
4. The system will display a green **CHECK-IN SUCCESSFUL** message showing total guest counts.
5. If an alumnus's phone battery died, use **Manual Attendee Lookup** to search their name and complete check-in.

### 2.5 Exporting Attendance Reports
1. Navigate to **Events & Get-Togethers** -> Click on your event.
2. Click **Export Attendance CSV**.
3. Download the full spreadsheet containing attendee names, admission numbers, guest counts, and check-in timestamps.
